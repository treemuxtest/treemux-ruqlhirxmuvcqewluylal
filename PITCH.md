LaunchGuard is a real-time AI incident commander for startups facing outages, security scares, and payment failures. Teams paste incident facts once, and LaunchGuard orchestrates three frontier models (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter) to produce a consensus-driven 60-minute response plan, internal/external communications, and risk blind-spot checks. Unlike a generic chatbot, it is structured around incident severity, operational constraints, and executive urgency, so outputs are immediately actionable in Slack war rooms and leadership updates. This makes LaunchGuard both a strong hackathon demo and a credible SaaS wedge into reliability, security, and compliance workflows where minutes matter and mistakes are expensive.
