LaunchGuard AI helps product teams ship AI features safely without slowing down release velocity. In one workflow, it runs parallel audits across OpenAI, Anthropic, and OpenRouter models, then computes a consensus risk profile, red-team misuse scenarios, and a concrete go-live checklist. What makes it unique is model disagreement visibility: teams can see exactly where providers diverge, which turns opaque AI judgment into an actionable launch decision. The demo is production-ready for startup teams because it outputs investor- and leadership-friendly launch briefs in seconds, making governance a competitive advantage instead of a bottleneck.
